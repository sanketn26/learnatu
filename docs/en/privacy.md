# Privacy

Learnatu is a static website: pages of text, the way a printed booklet is pages of text. It does not run a login, does not have a database of readers, and does not store anything about you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect your name, email, phone number, IP address, or how you browse.

Progress through a course (which lessons you marked complete) is saved only on your own phone or computer, in the browser. If you clear the browser’s storage, that tick-list goes with it. It never comes to us.

## Analytics

There are currently no analytics tools on this site. We are not counting your visits.

## Hosted by

The files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub’s own systems may log technical details as any web host does. See [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Cookies

This site does not set cookies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will appear on this page with a note at the top.
