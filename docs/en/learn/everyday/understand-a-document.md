# Understand a difficult document

AI can explain unfamiliar language, but uploading a real document may expose private information.

## Safer method

1. Identify the small paragraph or term you do not understand.
2. Copy only that general wording, without names, numbers, signatures, or identifying details.
3. Ask for a plain-language explanation and a list of questions to ask.
4. Compare the explanation with the original text.
5. Ask the responsible professional before signing or acting.

## Try this prompt

> Explain this general clause in simple language. Tell me what it appears to require, what is unclear, and which questions I should ask a qualified professional. Do not tell me whether to sign it: [non-private clause]

AI does not know the complete agreement, your circumstances, or the latest applicable law.
