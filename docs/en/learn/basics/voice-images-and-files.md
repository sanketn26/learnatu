# Voice, images, and file uploads

Speaking to AI can be easier than typing, and uploading a photo can save time. An upload is still a form of sharing.

## Before you upload

- Crop out faces, addresses, signatures, QR codes, and account numbers
- Remove document metadata when possible
- Check the background of photographs for private details
- Use a fictional or blank sample instead of a real document
- Confirm that your workplace permits the tool

Voice recordings can contain names, health details, background conversations, and location clues. Speak only what you would be comfortable typing.

Never upload identity documents, medical records, customer lists, private contracts, intimate images, keys, access codes, or security-system photographs to a public AI tool.
