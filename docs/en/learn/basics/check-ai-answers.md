# How to check an AI answer

AI can invent facts, sources, prices, rules, and quotations. Confidence is not proof.

## Use the three-check method

1. **Check the claim.** Which names, numbers, dates, rules, dosages, or prices could cause harm if wrong?
2. **Check the source.** Open the official website, original document, textbook, or trusted professional source yourself.
3. **Check the fit.** Even a true statement may not apply to your location, workplace, health, or circumstances.

Do not ask the same AI “Are you sure?” and count its confidence as verification. It may repeat the same error more confidently.
