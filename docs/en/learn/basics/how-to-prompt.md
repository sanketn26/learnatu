# How to ask AI a useful question

A good prompt does not need special technical words. Give the AI a clear job, useful context, boundaries, and the format you want.

## A simple formula

> Help me **[do the task]** for **[the audience or situation]**. Use **[language and tone]**. Include **[what is needed]**. Do not **[important boundary]**. Ask me before making assumptions.

## Example

> Turn these material and labour notes into a short customer quotation. Use polite, simple English. Separate materials and labour. Do not invent prices, dates, or guarantees. Replace personal information with placeholders.

## Improve the answer

You can say:

- “Make this shorter.”
- “Explain that word.”
- “Give me three alternatives.”
- “What assumptions did you make?”
- “List what I should verify.”

Before sending the prompt, remove names, phone numbers, addresses, account details, patient information, and workplace secrets.
