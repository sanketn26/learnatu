# What AI is—and is not

AI tools predict useful answers from patterns in large amounts of information. They can help you write, translate, explain, organise, and brainstorm. They do not think like a person, know your situation automatically, or guarantee the truth.

## Good uses

- Turn rough notes into a polite message
- Explain an unfamiliar word in simpler language
- Suggest questions you may have forgotten
- Create a first draft that you will review

## Poor uses

- Deciding whether a medical symptom is dangerous
- Confirming that a payment arrived
- Giving a final legal or financial decision
- Identifying whether a stranger is trustworthy

> **Remember:** A fluent answer is not necessarily a correct answer. Treat AI output as a draft, not a decision.
