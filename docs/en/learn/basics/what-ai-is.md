# What AI is — and is not

You type a question. A few seconds later, a complete-sounding answer appears. It may thank you, apologise, or even say “as a doctor” — as if a person is sitting on the other side.

There is not.

What you are talking to is a computer program that has read a huge amount of text and learned which words often come next. That is all “AI” means on this site: tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot that write, translate, and explain in ordinary language.

That is why they can turn rough notes into a polite message, or explain a difficult word. It is also why they can invent a medicine dose, a court case, or a helpline number that does not exist — and say it with the same calm confidence. Fluency is not the same as truth.

## What it is good for

Think of AI as a fast junior assistant who has never met you and will not go and check.

- Turn messy notes into a clearer message
- Explain an unfamiliar word in simpler language
- Suggest questions you may have forgotten to ask a teacher, a bank, or a doctor
- Give you a first draft that **you** will read and correct

Those are useful jobs. They save time. They do not require the tool to know your life.

## What it must not decide

- Whether a medical symptom is dangerous, or what dose of a medicine to take
- Whether money has actually arrived in your account
- A final legal, tax, or financial decision
- Whether a stranger on a call or a video is who they claim to be

The tool has not examined you, opened your bank app, or stood in the room. It is matching patterns from text it has seen before.

> **Remember:** A fluent answer is not necessarily a correct answer. Treat anything an AI writes as a draft. You make the decision.

## A small picture of how it goes wrong

Ananya asked an AI, “What is the emergency number for reporting cybercrime in India?” It answered with a number that sounded official. It was not **1930**. If she had dialled the invented number, she might have reached a scammer who was waiting for exactly that kind of confused call.

The fix is not “never use AI.” The fix is: for anything that would move money, change treatment, or go into a school assignment, [check the answer yourself](check-ai-answers.md).
