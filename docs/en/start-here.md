# Start here

Six steps to be safer online right now.

---

**Step 1 — Lock your phone**

Set a PIN, pattern, or fingerprint lock. Without it, anyone who picks up your phone can read everything.

**Step 2 — Only official apps**

Install apps only from the Play Store or App Store. Never install from a link someone sent you over WhatsApp or SMS.

**Step 3 — Never share your OTP**

Your bank, UPI app, or telecom will never call you and ask for an OTP. No one who asks for your OTP on a call is legitimate. End the call.

**Step 4 — One password habit**

Use a different password for your email than for everything else. Your email is the master key — if someone gets in, they can reset all your other passwords.

**Step 5 — Pause before you paste or tap**

Before you copy something from your work files into a chat box, or before you tap a link, ask: "Would I be okay if this was read by a stranger?" If no — stop.
