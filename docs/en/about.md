# About

Learnatu is a free, static handbook written by **Sanket Naik**.

It is designed for people who use smartphones every day but were never taught how to stay safe online — especially in India, where phone-first internet access is the norm.

## What this site is

- A plain-language guide to everyday digital safety
- Free to read, no account required, no ads
- Covers real apps: WhatsApp, UPI, Google, ChatGPT, and more
- Available in English and Hindi

## What this site is not

- Not legal advice
- Not medical advice
- Not banking advice
- Not a police helpline
- Not an "AI school" — AI is one app among many, treated the same way as WhatsApp or UPI

## Reporting cybercrime in India

If you have been scammed, report it at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in) or call the national helpline **1930**.
