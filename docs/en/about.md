# About

Learnatu is a free handbook written by **Sanket Naik**.

It is for people who use a smartphone every day and were never taught how to stay safe on it — especially in India, where the phone *is* the internet: WhatsApp for family, UPI for money, Google for questions, ChatGPT for drafting.

You should not need a computer-science class to keep your money, your photos, and your family information safe. You should not need English jargon to use an AI tool without handing it a bank statement.

## What this site is

- A plain-language guide to everyday digital safety and useful AI
- Free to read. No account. No ads
- Built around real apps: WhatsApp, UPI, Google, ChatGPT, and the rest
- Available in English and Hindi, with more Indian languages on the way

## What this site is not

- Not legal advice
- Not medical advice
- Not banking advice
- Not a police helpline
- Not an “AI school.” AI is one app among many, treated with the same caution as WhatsApp or UPI

If a page here disagrees with your doctor, your bank, your employer, or the law, those win.

## Reporting cybercrime in India

If you have been cheated online, report it at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in) or call the national helpline **1930**. The call is free. They handle this all day.
