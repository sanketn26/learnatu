# Impersonation, cloned voices, and “digital arrest”

A familiar voice, profile photo, uniform, caller ID, or video is not proof of identity. Images and voices can be copied or generated.

## Verify using another channel

1. End the unexpected call.
2. Call the person back using a number you already saved.
3. Ask a question only the real person would know.
4. Contact the organisation through its official published number.

No legitimate authority keeps you under “digital arrest” over a video call or asks you to transfer money to prove innocence. Urgent secrecy, threats, and instructions to stay on the call are danger signs.

Do not share your screen, move to a private room, or transfer funds while someone is pressuring you.
