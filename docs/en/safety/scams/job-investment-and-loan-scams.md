# Job, investment, and loan scams

The message is flattering. Work from home. Guaranteed returns. Instant loan, no documents. Sometimes they even pay you a little at the start, so the story feels real. That small payment is not kindness. It is bait.

Scammers sell a feeling: at last, a door opened. The early steps look like onboarding. Then a fee appears — to “unlock” earnings, to “verify” the account, to “release” the loan.

## Pause when you see

- Money required *before* a job, a withdrawal, or a loan is released
- “Guaranteed profit” or “zero risk.” Nobody honest can promise the market, or a job, will only go up
- Tasks that are mostly ratings, fake orders, or putting money into cryptocurrency (digital tokens whose value can vanish)
- Pressure to borrow so you can unlock what you “already earned”
- Requests for your contacts, photos, identity documents, or control of your screen
- All conversation happening only in a private Telegram or WhatsApp group, never on a company website you typed yourself

Check the organisation the slow way. Search its name plus “register” or look it up on the official regulator’s site. Do not use the recruiter’s own link as proof that they exist.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Pay money upfront for a job, a withdrawal, or an “unlock” fee.
    - Borrow or send more money to recover an earlier payment.
    - Share identity documents, contacts, photos, or screen access with a recruiter or promoter you have not verified yourself.

A request to send more money “to recover” what you already paid is usually the next chapter of the same scam, not the way out of it. Walk away from the sunk money if you have to. Sending more is how the hole gets deeper.
