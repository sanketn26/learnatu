# Job, investment, and loan scams

Scammers promise easy work, guaranteed returns, instant loans, or special access. The early steps may appear genuine and may even include a small payment.

## Pause when you see

- Payment required before a job or withdrawal
- Guaranteed profit or “zero risk”
- Tasks involving ratings, orders, or cryptocurrency deposits
- Pressure to borrow money to unlock earnings
- Requests for contacts, photos, identity documents, or screen access
- Communication only through private messaging groups

Check the organisation independently. Search for its official registration and contact details; do not use links supplied by the recruiter or promoter.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Pay money upfront for a job, withdrawal, or "unlock" fee.
    - Borrow or send more money to recover an earlier payment.
    - Share identity documents, contacts, photos, or screen access with a recruiter or promoter.

A request to send more money "to recover" what you already paid is usually the next stage of the same scam, not a way out of it.
