# Remote-access and fake support scams

**Remote access** means another person can see your screen, and sometimes tap it, from their own computer. Apps such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, and “Quick Support” do this. They have honest uses — a company’s IT team helping an employee who asked. They are also a favourite of criminals who phone you first.

The story is always that they are already on your side: bank fraud team, police cyber cell, courier, telecom, Windows support. They need you to install one small app “so we can fix it.” Then they watch you open UPI. Then they ask for a “test” payment or an OTP.

## Warning signs

- An unexpected caller wants you to install an app
- They ask you to share the screen while you are in a banking or payment app
- They tell you not to talk to family, or to go to a quiet room
- They ask for an OTP, PIN, card detail, or “test payment”
- You found their number through a search advertisement, not through the app you already have or the back of your card

End the call. Find support through the official app, the number on the card, or an address you typed yourself. Real bank staff do not begin a relationship by taking your screen.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Install a remote-access app because an unexpected caller asked you to.
    - Share your screen, an OTP, a PIN, or a card detail during a call about banking or “test payments.”
    - Hide a call from your family or from bank staff because the caller told you to.

If you already installed remote-access software: airplane mode, uninstall the app, check money from another trusted device, call the bank if anything was on that screen. [I installed a suspicious app](../../help/suspicious-app.md) has the slower checklist.
