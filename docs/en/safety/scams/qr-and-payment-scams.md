# QR codes and payment scams

A QR code can open a website or prepare a payment. Scanning a code is not proof that money will come to you.

## Rules that prevent loss

- You normally do not enter a UPI PIN to receive money
- Read the amount and “paying to” name before approving
- Confirm received money inside your own bank or UPI app
- Do not trust screenshots, SMS messages, or a caller's screen
- Never scan a code sent by someone claiming it will issue a refund

If a buyer says they “accidentally paid extra,” check your own account before returning anything. Fake payment screens and edited screenshots are common persuasion tools.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
    - Scan a code sent by someone claiming it will issue a refund.
    - Trust a screenshot, SMS, or a caller's screen as proof of payment.
