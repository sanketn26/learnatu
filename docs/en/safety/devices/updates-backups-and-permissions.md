# Updates, backups, and permissions

Three quiet habits prevent many digital emergencies.

## Keep software updated

Install phone and app updates from system settings or the official app store. Updates often repair security weaknesses.

## Keep a backup

Back up important contacts and photographs to an account you protect with a unique password and two-step verification. Check occasionally that recovery works.

## Limit permissions

Review camera, microphone, contacts, files, and location access. A permission should match the app's purpose. A torch or calculator does not need your contacts.

Delete apps you no longer use. Do not install “cleaner,” “booster,” or update apps from advertisements or message links.
