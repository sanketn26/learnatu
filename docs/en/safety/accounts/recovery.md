# Secure account recovery

“Recovery” is how you get back in when you forget a password or lose a phone. It is also how an attacker gets in if they can control the email or number you listed as backup.

Treat recovery details with the same care as the password. Your email account is often the master key to everything else: **Forgot password?** on other sites sends a link there.

## Prepare before trouble

- Use a recovery email that **you** open, not a shared family inbox you never check
- Keep the recovery phone number current — an old SIM you gave away is a spare key walking around
- Store backup codes on paper or in a place that is not the same phone
- Remove old phones and unknown sessions from the account
- Write down the official recovery page for the accounts you cannot afford to lose (type it once, on paper). When panic hits, you will not want to search ads

## If you lose access

Use only the recovery option inside the official app, or a website address you typed yourself. Ignore people in comments, messages, or search advertisements who promise to recover an account for a fee. They already know you are desperate. That is the product they sell.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Trust a stranger in comments or ads who offers to recover your account for a fee.
    - Use a recovery email or phone number you do not personally control.
    - Leave old phones or unknown sessions signed in after you get back in.

After recovery: change the password, sign out other sessions, check forwarding rules and recovery details, and turn on [two-step verification](two-step-verification.md). Getting back in is only half the job. Closing the door behind you is the rest.
