# Secure account recovery

Your email account is often the master key to everything else. Recovery details deserve the same protection as your password.

## Prepare before trouble

- Use a recovery email that you control
- Keep your recovery phone number current
- Store backup codes securely and offline
- Remove old phones and unknown sessions
- Write down the official recovery page for critical accounts

## If you lose access

Use only the recovery option inside the official app or a website address you typed yourself. Ignore people in comments, messages, or search advertisements who promise to recover an account for a fee.

After recovery, change the password, sign out other sessions, check forwarding rules and recovery details, and turn on two-step verification.
