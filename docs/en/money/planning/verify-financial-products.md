# Verify a financial product or adviser

Professional-looking websites, certificates, apps, and testimonials can be copied. Verify independently before sharing documents or money.

## Verification steps

1. Identify which regulator or authority should cover the product or provider.
2. Search that authority's official register yourself.
3. Confirm the exact legal name, registration details, website, phone number, and payment account.
4. Read fees, risks, lock-ins, exit rules, and complaint procedures.
5. Take time. Refuse secrecy, urgency, remote access, and guaranteed returns.

Do not use a registration link or phone number supplied only by the seller as your verification source.

AI may help you create a checklist, but it cannot certify that a provider is genuine. For India, start with the relevant official resources from [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/), [SEBI Investor](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/), [IRDAI](https://irdai.gov.in/), or [PFRDA](https://www.pfrda.org.in/).
