# Start planning for retirement

Retirement planning means preparing for future living costs when regular work income reduces or stops. Starting small and consistently can be more practical than waiting for a perfect plan.

## Build the picture

- Expected essential living and healthcare costs
- Existing pensions, retirement accounts, savings, property, and debts
- People who may depend on you
- Time remaining and how inflation may affect costs
- Access, risk, fees, and tax treatment of any product considered

## A realistic example

Someone earning ₹40,000 a month plans to retire in 25 years. If essential monthly costs today are ₹25,000, inflation alone could push that above ₹1,00,000 a month by retirement. Starting a small, consistent contribution now — even a modest amount — compounds over 25 years far better than waiting 10 years to start a larger one. The exact numbers depend on your own income, costs, and chosen products, but the principle holds: time matters more than the size of the first contribution.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Move retirement savings immediately because of a call, message, or urgent deadline.
    - Choose a product because of a guaranteed high-return promise — no legitimate pension product can guarantee market returns.
    - Sign paperwork you have not read in full, including fee structure and lock-in period.

AI can produce questions and scenarios, but it cannot know future returns, inflation, lifespan, regulation, or your complete needs. For official Indian pension information, consult [PFRDA](https://www.pfrda.org.in/); use a qualified professional for individual decisions when necessary.
