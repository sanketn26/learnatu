# Save, invest, or repay debt?

These choices serve different purposes. The right order depends on urgency, cost, time, and risk—not on what is popular online.

## Questions to ask

- Will I need this money soon?
- Could I tolerate its value falling?
- What interest and fees am I paying on debt?
- Do I have accessible money for emergencies?
- Do I understand how this product works and how I can exit?
- Is the provider properly regulated for what it offers?

Saving usually prioritises access and stability. Investing accepts uncertainty for a longer-term goal. Repaying expensive debt can reduce a known cost. None of these automatically wins in every situation.

Never invest because an AI tool, influencer, messaging group, or stranger promises a particular return. Use [SEBI Investor](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/) for Indian investor education and verification resources.
