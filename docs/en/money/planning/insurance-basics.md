# Understand insurance

Insurance transfers a defined financial risk; it does not remove every risk or guarantee every claim.

## Before buying

1. Identify what event you need protection from.
2. Read who and what is covered.
3. Check exclusions, waiting periods, limits, deductibles, and renewal conditions.
4. Understand the premium and what happens if payments stop.
5. Disclose required information truthfully.
6. Verify the insurer and intermediary independently.

Do not rely only on a sales illustration, verbal promise, or AI summary. Read the official policy wording and ask for unclear points in writing.

AI can simplify general terminology after private details are removed, but it should not decide which policy you buy. Indian consumers can consult [IRDAI](https://irdai.gov.in/) for policyholder information and official resources.
