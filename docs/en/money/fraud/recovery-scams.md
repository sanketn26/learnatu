# Beware of recovery scams

After a loss, criminals may contact you again pretending to be investigators, lawyers, hackers, bank staff, or recovery agents. They know details of the first fraud and use them to sound credible.

## They may request

- An investigation, tax, release, or processing fee
- Remote access to your phone
- Account login, OTP, PIN, or recovery code
- Another transfer to trace or unlock the first payment
- Secrecy about their “special process”

Do not send more money. Report through your bank and official authorities using contact information you find independently.

In India, report financial cybercrime promptly through **1930** and [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in). No private person can guarantee recovery.
