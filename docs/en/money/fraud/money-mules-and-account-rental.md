# Never rent or lend your bank account

A stranger or acquaintance may offer money for receiving and forwarding funds, sharing a bank account, opening an account, converting money, or providing a SIM. This can make your identity and account part of a fraud chain.

## Warning phrases

- “Keep a commission and send the rest”
- “We only need your account for company payments”
- “Receive customer money while working from home”
- “Open an account or wallet for us”
- “Convert these funds to cryptocurrency”

Do not share account access, cards, SIMs, OTPs, PINs, identity documents, or signed blank forms. Do not move suspicious money onward.

If unexpected money arrives with instructions to forward it, contact your bank through an official channel and preserve the messages. Do not negotiate privately with the sender.
