# Fake payments and refund tricks

A screenshot, SMS, email, or caller's screen can be fabricated. Only your own verified account can confirm that money arrived.

## Common versions

- A buyer shows a fake successful-payment screen
- Someone claims to have paid too much and requests a refund
- A caller sends a QR code to “receive” or “reverse” money
- A small genuine payment is used to gain trust before a larger request
- A refund requires a PIN, OTP, screen share, or remote-access app

Open your bank or payment app yourself and check the completed transaction and available balance. Do not use a link in the message.

Never return an “extra payment” until your provider independently confirms that cleared money was actually received and explains the correct process.
