# Build an emergency fund

An emergency fund is money kept accessible for unexpected essential costs such as urgent travel, a repair, temporary income loss, or medical needs. It is not meant for high returns.

## Start without an intimidating target

1. Choose a small first milestone.
2. Keep it separate from everyday spending when practical.
3. Add a manageable amount after income arrives.
4. Refill it after using it.
5. Increase the target gradually as your situation allows.

Someone with variable income, dependants, health needs, or unstable work may need a different reserve from someone with predictable income. Avoid borrowing or skipping essential needs merely to reach a generic target.

Keep emergency money in a place you understand, can access when needed, and have independently verified. “Guaranteed high-return emergency funds” are a contradiction and a warning sign.
