# Understand a loan before accepting it

An affordable-looking instalment can hide a large total cost. Compare the complete obligation, not only the monthly EMI.

## Ask for these facts in writing

- Amount you actually receive
- Interest rate and how it is calculated
- Every processing, insurance, platform, and late fee
- EMI amount and number of payments
- Total amount repaid
- Consequences of missed or early payments
- Whether security or a guarantor is involved

## A realistic example

A loan app offers ₹50,000 with a monthly instalment of ₹4,800 for 12 months. That looks like ₹57,600 total — but the app also lists a ₹2,500 processing fee and a mandatory ₹1,800 insurance add-on taken out of the amount you receive. Your real amount received is ₹45,700, while your real total repaid is ₹57,600. The true cost is higher than the EMI alone suggests. Always calculate cost against what you actually receive, not the amount advertised.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Pay an advance fee to "release" or "unlock" a loan.
    - Share an OTP or let an app access your contacts without a clear, legitimate reason.
    - Install a lending app from a link in a message rather than an official app store.

AI can explain general loan terms, but it may calculate incorrectly or miss contract conditions. Verify calculations and terms against the lender's official written disclosure. For Indian financial education and alerts, consult the [Reserve Bank of India](https://www.rbi.org.in/).
