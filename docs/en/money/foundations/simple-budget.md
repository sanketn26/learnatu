# Make a simple budget

A budget is a plan for decisions, not a punishment. Start with the money you actually have and the responsibilities you actually carry.

## A practical order

1. Cover essential needs and minimum required payments.
2. Set aside something for irregular expenses.
3. Build a small emergency reserve.
4. Decide what remains for flexible spending and longer-term goals.
5. Review what actually happened at the end of the month.

When income changes, create a “minimum month” plan and decide in advance how extra income will be divided.

## Safe AI prompt

> Create a blank monthly budget template for a household with variable income. Use categories only. Do not ask for account numbers, names, addresses, or exact bank records.

No budgeting formula fits every household. The useful budget is the one you can understand, adjust, and follow.
