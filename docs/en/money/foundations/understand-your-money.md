# Understand your money

Financial planning starts with knowing what comes in, what must go out, and what changes from month to month. It does not require a complicated spreadsheet.

## Make a one-month picture

Write down:

1. **Income:** salary, wages, business income, pension, benefits, and other reliable income.
2. **Essential expenses:** food, housing, utilities, transport, medicine, school costs, and minimum debt payments.
3. **Flexible expenses:** purchases that can change or wait.
4. **Irregular expenses:** annual fees, repairs, festivals, insurance, and seasonal costs divided into monthly amounts.

If income varies, plan using a cautious lower-income month rather than the best month.

AI can help organise anonymous categories, but do not upload bank statements, account numbers, payslips, tax documents, or identity records to a public AI tool.

> **Outcome:** I can describe where my money comes from and where it needs to go.
