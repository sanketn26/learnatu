# Employees

You use apps every day at work — email, chat, document editors, and increasingly AI tools like ChatGPT or Gemini. This guide covers the one question that matters most: what is safe to paste in, and what is not.

## The two principles that matter most for employees

**Principle 2 — Secrets stay off chat.**
Customer names, phone numbers, payroll figures, contracts, and source code should not go into a personal AI account. Your company's data is not yours to share.

**Principle 1 — Pause before you paste.**
Before copying from a work document and pasting into any online tool, ask: does my employer know I am doing this? Would they be comfortable?
