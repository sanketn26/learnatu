# Customer data

**Who this is for:** Small business owners who collect customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or payment details.

**Risk level:**

!!! danger "Never upload"
    Customer names, phone numbers, and order details must not be uploaded to personal AI accounts, WhatsApp groups, or any platform outside your own devices without clear reason and customer consent.

---

## What could go wrong

- You paste a customer list into ChatGPT to "sort it" — that data is now on a third-party server.
- You share a list of customer phone numbers in a group chat — one member uses it for unsolicited marketing.
- Your phone is stolen with an unprotected spreadsheet of 500 customer orders.
- A delivery app you used retained customer addresses and sold them to a competitor.
- India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) requires you to protect customer data. A breach can mean complaints, fines, or loss of customer trust.

---

## A safe prompt

Do not use AI here with real data. For learning, use fake data:

> "Here is a sample customer list with fake names: [list]. Help me sort it by city."

When you are done learning the format, apply it yourself to your real data locally.

---

## How to verify you are handling data safely

1. Ask: is this customer data on my own device, or on someone else's server?
2. If you are using a cloud tool (Google Sheets, etc.), check who has access to the file.
3. If a vendor asks for your customer list, ask why they need it and what they will do with it.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Is my customer spreadsheet password-protected or in a locked app?
- [ ] Have I avoided pasting real customer names or numbers into any AI tool?
- [ ] Do I know where all copies of my customer list are?
- [ ] Have I deleted customer data I no longer need?
- [ ] Does my business have a simple privacy notice for customers (even one sentence on your invoice)?

---

## Privacy settings to use

- **Google Sheets:** Share with specific people only — not "anyone with the link."
- **WhatsApp Business:** Do not store customer lists in broadcast groups that include non-staff members.
- **Phone:** Enable screen lock and encrypt your device (Android: Settings → Security → Encryption).
- **Email:** Use a business email address, not a personal Gmail, for customer communications.
- **Backup:** Store customer data backups on a physical drive you own, not only in a free cloud account.

---

## A realistic failure

Deepak runs a small pharmacy and kept his regular customers' names, addresses, and medicines ordered in a Google Sheet shared via a "anyone with link" URL. He sent the link to a delivery partner. That partner shared it with a third person who ran a competing pharmacy. Deepak's customers started getting calls from a service they never contacted. If Deepak had set the sheet to "Specific people" instead of "Anyone with the link," the data would have stayed within his team.
