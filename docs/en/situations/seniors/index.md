## The two principles that matter most for seniors

**Principle 5 — If someone rushes you, it is probably a scam.**
A real bank, police officer, or government official will never call you and demand immediate payment or an OTP. Urgency is the scam's main weapon.

**Principle 3 — Official app and official number only.**
The bank's number is on the back of your card or on the bank's official website. The government's number is on the official `.gov.in` website. Trust those — not a number that called you.
