# Plumbers and electricians

Use AI to communicate clearly, prepare drafts faster, and explain your work—while keeping customer information and payments safe.

## A useful learning path

1. **Write a quotation.** Ask AI to turn your list of materials and labour into a clear quotation. Replace the customer's real name, phone number, and address with placeholders.
2. **Explain the work.** Ask for a simple explanation of the repair that a customer can understand.
3. **Translate a message.** Draft the message without private details, translate it, and read it once before sending.
4. **Check every payment yourself.** Never trust a screenshot. Open your own bank or UPI app and confirm that the money arrived.

## Try this prompt

> Turn these notes into a polite quotation. Separate materials and labour. Use simple language. Do not add prices or promises that I did not provide: [paste notes without customer details]

## Never share with AI

- A customer's phone number or home address
- Aadhaar, PAN, bank, card, OTP, or UPI PIN details
- Photos showing keys, access codes, security systems, or private rooms
- A payment screenshot as proof that money arrived

AI produces a draft. You remain responsible for checking prices, measurements, safety requirements, and promises made to the customer.
