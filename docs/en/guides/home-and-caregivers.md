# Homemakers and family caregivers

AI can help with household communication, learning, planning, and understanding unfamiliar information. It should not make family health, financial, or safety decisions.

## Useful tasks

- Translate a school or service message after removing names and numbers
- Turn a shopping list into a simple weekly plan
- Explain a difficult general term from a bill or form
- Create questions to ask a teacher, bank, doctor, or service provider
- Draft a polite complaint without sharing account details

## Stay safe

Do not upload family identity documents, medical records, children's photographs, school details, addresses, bills containing account numbers, or private conversations.

Check prices with the actual seller, health information with a qualified professional, and official requirements on the relevant organisation's website.
