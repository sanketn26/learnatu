# Teachers and job seekers

AI can help prepare drafts and practise skills. It should not impersonate your work or receive other people's private information.

## For teachers

- Create example questions using fictional students
- Rewrite general instructions at a simpler reading level
- Brainstorm activities, then check accuracy and suitability
- Never upload student work, names, grades, accommodations, or contact details without approval

## For job seekers

- Improve a résumé after replacing personal details with placeholders
- Practise interview questions
- Draft a cover letter based on your real experience
- Verify every employer independently

Never pay to unlock a job, interview, task income, or equipment shipment. Do not let AI invent qualifications, employers, achievements, or references.
