# Gig and delivery workers

Use AI for communication and planning, but keep employer, customer, location, and payment data private.

## Useful tasks

- Rewrite a support message clearly
- Translate a non-private customer message
- Prepare questions about a contract or policy
- Organise general expense categories without uploading statements

## Common risks

Beware of fake onboarding fees, task scams, account-verification links, screen-sharing requests, and callers asking for OTPs. Open the official worker app yourself for support.

Never paste customer names, addresses, phone numbers, order details, live location, IDs, payslips, bank statements, or employer-only information into a public AI tool.
