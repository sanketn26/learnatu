# Passwords

A strong password is your first line of defence. You do not need to memorise twenty of them — you need one good habit.

---

## Steps to a safer password

1. **Your email password must be unique.** It is the master key. If someone resets your email, they can reset everything else.
2. **Make it a sentence, not a word.** `MyDogRaju@2024!` is far stronger than `raju123`.
3. **Never reuse a password across important accounts** (bank, email, UPI app).
4. **Change a password the moment you think someone may have seen it.**
5. **Do not write passwords on paper near your phone or computer.**

---

!!! danger "Never"
    - Share a password over WhatsApp, SMS, or a call.
    - Use your date of birth, phone number, or child's name as a password.
    - Save passwords in a chat with yourself on WhatsApp.

---

## If you already reused a password

Change the one on your email account first, then change it wherever you used the same password. Start with banking apps.

---

## A note on password managers

Password manager apps (like Google's built-in one in Chrome/Android) store your passwords securely. They are safer than reusing the same password. If you use one, protect it with your phone's fingerprint lock.
