# Fake links and fake apps

Fraudsters build websites and apps that look exactly like real ones. One wrong tap can empty your account.

---

## Steps to spot a fake link

1. **Read the full URL carefully.** `hdfc-bank-refund.in` is not HDFC Bank. The real site is `hdfcbank.com`.
2. **Look for misspellings.** `gpay-support.net`, `goog1e.com`, `whatsapp-update.com` — these are all fake.
3. **A link inside a message is always suspicious.** Type the address yourself into the browser instead of tapping.
4. **Check the security padlock.** A padlock (https) means the connection is encrypted — it does NOT mean the site is genuine. Scam sites also have padlocks.
5. **Do not trust a logo.** Anyone can copy a logo and paste it onto a webpage.

---

!!! danger "Never"
    - Tap "Install" from a link sent on WhatsApp, SMS, or email.
    - Log in to your bank on a site you reached through a message link.
    - Enter your OTP, PIN, or password on a page you are not 100% sure is genuine.

---

## How to verify a link is real

- Type the bank or service's name in Google and use the result from the official website listed (check the URL).
- Call the bank's official helpline (on the back of your card) and ask.
- Ask a younger family member who is comfortable with technology.

---

## If you already tapped and entered details

1. Change your password on the real website immediately.
2. Call your bank (number on card) if any payment information was entered.
3. Report at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in) or call **1930**.
