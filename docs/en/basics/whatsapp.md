# WhatsApp

WhatsApp is how most of India communicates. It is also the most common channel for scams and misinformation.

---

## Steps

1. **Enable two-step verification.** Go to Settings → Account → Two-step verification. Add a 6-digit PIN. This stops someone from moving your number to their device.
2. **Control who can see your profile photo and status.** Settings → Privacy → Profile photo → My contacts.
3. **Do not add strangers to your contacts.** A person must not see your profile just because they have your number.
4. **Forwarded messages are not news.** Before you forward a message, ask: can I verify this from a news website I trust?
5. **Calls from unknown international numbers are often scams.** +92, +1, +44 — if you do not know the person, do not answer.

---

!!! danger "Never"
    - Share OTP over WhatsApp with anyone.
    - Click "Install" or "Download" links received in a WhatsApp message.
    - Send Aadhaar photo, PAN photo, or bank details over WhatsApp — even to people you know, unless truly necessary.

---

## Privacy settings to use

- **Profile photo:** My contacts
- **Last seen:** My contacts or Nobody
- **Status:** My contacts
- **Groups:** My contacts
- **Two-step verification:** On (Settings → Account)
- **Read receipts:** Your choice, but keeping them on helps confirm messages were received

---

## If you already tapped a bad link

1. Do not enter any details on the page that opened.
2. Close the browser tab.
3. Clear your browser cache (Settings → Apps → Browser → Clear data).
4. If you entered any details, change those passwords immediately.
