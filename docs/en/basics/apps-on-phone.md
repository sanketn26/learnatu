# Apps on your phone

Every app you install is a small program that runs on your phone and can access your camera, contacts, and location — if you allow it.

---

## Steps

1. **Only install from Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone).** Never install from a link someone sent you on WhatsApp, SMS, or email.
2. **Check the developer name before installing.** The official BHIM app is by NPCI. Fake apps copy the logo but have a different developer.
3. **Review permissions when the app asks.** A torch app does not need your contacts. If it asks, deny it.
4. **Delete apps you do not use.** Unused apps can still collect data and receive updates with new permissions.
5. **Keep your phone and apps updated.** Updates often fix security holes.

---

!!! danger "Never install"
    - APK files from WhatsApp, Telegram, or any website.
    - Any app that promises "double your money," "free recharge," or "government cash."
    - Remote access tools (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, Quick Support) unless your company's IT team is guiding you step by step on a work computer — not your personal phone.

---

## If you already installed something suspicious

1. Uninstall it immediately.
2. Check your recent bank and UPI transactions.
3. Change passwords for banking and email apps.
4. If you used the app, report it at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in).
