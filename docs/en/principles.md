# Guiding principles

Eight rules that cover almost every situation. Read them once and you will recognise a scam before it harms you.

---

1. **Pause before you tap, paste, or pay.**
   One second of hesitation is worth more than an hour of regret.

2. **Secrets stay off chat.**
   Never type OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, passwords, Aadhaar photo, backup codes, customer lists, or unpublished contracts into any chat box, AI tool, or web form that you did not open yourself.

3. **Official app and official number only.**
   Do not install any app from a link in a message. Open the Play Store or App Store yourself. If you need to call support, use the number on the back of your card or the official website.

4. **Treat every confident answer — human or AI — as a draft. Check it.**
   ChatGPT, Google, WhatsApp forwards, and well-meaning relatives can all be wrong. Verify important information from an official source.

5. **If someone rushes you, it is probably a scam.**
   "Pay in the next 10 minutes or you will be arrested" is always a scam. Urgency is a tool fraudsters use. Slow down.

6. **Share the minimum. Default is "do not upload."**
   Before you share a photo, document, or piece of information, ask whether the other person truly needs it.

7. **You can refuse. Closing the chat is a valid action.**
   You do not owe a reply to a stranger on WhatsApp. You do not have to answer every question an AI asks. You are in control.

8. **Teach the person next to you the same rule.**
   A scam that fails on you will be tried on someone else. Share one rule at a time with family or colleagues.
