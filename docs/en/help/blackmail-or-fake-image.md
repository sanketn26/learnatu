# Someone is threatening me or sharing a fake image

This is not your fault. Do not handle it alone.

1. Do not pay, send more images, or meet the person.
2. Save evidence: usernames, profile links, messages, dates, and screenshots.
3. Ask a trusted person to help you report the account and content.
4. Use the platform's impersonation, harassment, or intimate-image reporting process.
5. Report threats or non-consensual intimate content to the appropriate local authority. In India, use **cybercrime.gov.in** when applicable.
6. If there is immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services.

Avoid repeatedly forwarding the harmful content as evidence. Preserve it securely and share it only with people handling the report.
