# I installed a suspicious app

1. Turn on airplane mode or disconnect Wi-Fi and mobile data.
2. Do not open banking, email, password-manager, or payment apps on that phone.
3. Uninstall the suspicious app and remove unusual accessibility, device-admin, screen-sharing, or notification access.
4. From another trusted device, change critical passwords and review transactions.
5. Contact your bank immediately if financial information or screen access was exposed.
6. Back up essential personal files and consider a factory reset if control of the phone is uncertain.

Do not call a removal number displayed by the suspicious app. Use official support channels only.
