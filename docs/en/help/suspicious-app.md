# I installed a suspicious app

Treat the phone as if someone else may be able to watch it until the app is gone. Do not open banking, email, or payment apps on it while the stranger-program is still there.

1. Turn on airplane mode, or switch off Wi-Fi and mobile data, so the app cannot send anything further.
2. Leave bank, email, password-manager, and UPI apps closed on that phone.
3. Uninstall the suspicious app. Then check **Settings** for leftover permissions: accessibility (full control of the screen), device admin, screen sharing, or notification access. Turn those off for anything you do not recognise.
4. From another trusted device — a family member’s phone, a computer — change important passwords and look at recent transactions.
5. Call your bank if you entered a PIN, an OTP, card details, or let someone see the screen.
6. Back up photos you cannot lose. If you are still not sure who controls the phone, a factory reset (erase and set up again) is the clean end.

!!! danger "Never"
    - Open banking, email, password-manager, or payment apps on a phone with a suspicious app still installed.
    - Call a “removal” number displayed by the suspicious app itself.
    - Leave accessibility, device-admin, or screen-sharing access on for an app you do not fully trust.

Help removing it comes from the official Play Store or App Store, or from a person you already trust — never from a number the fake app painted on your screen.
