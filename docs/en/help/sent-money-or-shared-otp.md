# I sent money or shared an OTP

Act immediately. Do not spend time arguing with the scammer.

1. Contact your bank or payment provider through its official app or number.
2. Ask them to secure the account and record the fraudulent transaction.
3. In India, call **1930** promptly and submit a report at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
4. Save transaction IDs, numbers, messages, and screenshots. Do not edit them.
5. Change affected passwords from a trusted device.
6. Tell a trusted person who can help you follow through.

Never pay a “recovery agent.” Do not send another payment to unlock, reverse, investigate, or refund the first one.

If you shared a UPI PIN, card PIN, password, or recovery code, tell the relevant provider exactly what was exposed.
