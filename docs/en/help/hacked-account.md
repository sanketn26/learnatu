# My account may be hacked

Use a trusted device if possible.

1. Open the official app or type its website address yourself.
2. Change the password to a new, unique one.
3. Sign out all other sessions and remove unknown devices.
4. Check recovery email, phone number, forwarding rules, and connected apps.
5. Turn on two-step verification and save recovery codes.
6. Warn contacts if the account sent suspicious requests.

If you cannot sign in, use only the platform's official recovery page. People replying on social media with “recovery experts” are often scammers.

Secure your primary email first when several accounts are affected, because it may be used to reset the others.
