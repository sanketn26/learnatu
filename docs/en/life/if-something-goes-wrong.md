# After a mistake

Everyone makes mistakes. The key is acting quickly when something goes wrong.

---

## Money was transferred to the wrong person or a scammer

1. **Act within minutes.** Call your bank immediately on the number on the back of your card.
2. Ask them to initiate a "chargeback" or freeze on the transaction.
3. File a complaint at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in) or call **1930** within 24 hours.
4. Note down: the amount, time, UPI ID or account number you sent to, and the app you used.

---

## You shared an OTP or PIN

1. Call your bank immediately. Ask them to block the card or account.
2. Change your UPI PIN from the official UPI app.
3. Check your recent transactions for any you did not make.
4. Report at cybercrime.gov.in.

---

## You clicked a suspicious link or installed something

1. Disconnect from Wi-Fi and mobile data briefly.
2. Uninstall the app.
3. Change passwords for your email and banking apps.
4. Check recent transactions.
5. If you believe remote access was given (e.g. AnyDesk), do a factory reset after backing up your photos.

---

## A photo or video of you was shared without your consent

1. Report the post to the platform (WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook) using the Report button.
2. Screenshot the URL as evidence before reporting.
3. Contact the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal: [cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in).
4. If the content is sexual in nature, this is a serious crime under the IT Act. Contact the police.

---

## You shared work or customer data in an AI tool by mistake

1. Delete the conversation if the tool allows it (ChatGPT → three dots on the chat → Delete).
2. Inform your manager as soon as possible. Honesty early is far better than a discovery later.
3. Check the AI tool's data retention policy and submit a data deletion request if available.

---

## General reminder

Mistakes happen to careful people too. The worst outcome is usually delay — the faster you act, the more can be recovered. You are not alone: the national helpline **1930** is free and handles thousands of such calls every day.
